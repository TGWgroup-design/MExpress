What each status means — the scenario behind it
Verified & accepted

M.Express has checked the claim; the client has accepted the 7% fee. Identity, bank details and the duplicate-claim check are done. The client has agreed to the advance terms but hasn’t been paid yet — this is the step just before M.Express releases money.

Client paid

M.Express has paid the client their 93% advance. The EFT is out. M.Express is now waiting on the client to confirm receipt before the vehicle-return step can begin.

Vehicle returned

The client has acknowledged payment and returned the vehicle. The hiring partner is inspecting it and assessing any deductions (fuel, tolls, e-tags, fines, damage) before confirming a final approved amount.

Reimbursement due

The partner has confirmed the final amount; M.Express has sent the reimbursement pack. M.Express is waiting on the partner’s EFT, due within 7 days of the pack being issued.

Reimbursed

The partner’s EFT has landed and been matched to this claim. The money is in; the claim just needs its final reconciliation sign-off to close.

Settled

Fully reconciled and closed. If deductions brought the final amount below what the client was advanced, the Shortfall column shows what M.Express is recovering from the client — that recovery can still be outstanding even once the claim itself is settled.

Overdue reimbursement

The partner missed its 7-day reimbursement deadline. Client payouts on that partner’s other claims still go ahead as normal, but this partner’s exposure limit for new claims is reduced until it pays. Currently only happening with First Car Rental.
